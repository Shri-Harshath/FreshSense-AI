# FreshSense AI - Smart Food Preservation & Freshness Monitoring System

FreshSense AI is an intelligent food preservation platform combining IoT telemetry analysis, spoilage kinetics, and Gemini Vision AI for zero-waste culinary guidance.

## Features
- **Overall Freshness Index**: Multi-sensor decay estimation (Temperature, Humidity, VOC/Gas PPM).
- **Food Profiles Registry**: Individual biological tolerances for Tomatoes, Lemon, Bell Pepper, Apple, Banana, and Leafy Greens.
- **Multimodal AI Vision & Recipe Copilot**: Google Gemini multimodal analysis for surface diagnostics and instant zero-waste recipes.
- **IoT Simulation Sandbox**: Real-time rapid spoilage scenarios with zero physical hardware required.
- **Interactive Analytics**: 24h, 7d, and 30d environmental and freshness trend tracking.
